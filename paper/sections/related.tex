% Section: Related Work

\paragraph*{Dynamic stopping in BCI}
Verschore et al.~\cite{Verschore2012} introduced classifier-confidence
thresholds for early termination on a P300 speller; Bianchi et
al.~\cite{BianchiLiti2019P300} proposed a refined early-stopping
decision rule in the same paradigm. Liu et
al.~\cite{Liu2017MotorImageryBTSPRT} adapted a sequential
probability ratio test (BTSPRT) to motor-imagery BCI. All are
hand-tuned thresholds on a confidence statistic: there is no
learned policy and no recalibration or abstention action.

\paragraph*{Sequential decision frameworks for BCI}
\textsc{MarkovType}~\cite{Sunger2024MarkovType} casts a P300 RSVP
speller as a POMDP solved with on-policy REINFORCE.
\textsc{EEG\_RL-Net}~\cite{Aung2024EEG_RL_Net} trains a Dueling DQN
classification head on a GNN backbone, online and without a
\textsc{Defer} action. ErrP-driven online
RL~\cite{Fidencio2025ErrPRL} adapts a decoder during a
paced game using error-related potentials as the reward signal. None
of these are \emph{offline} RL; none use a \textsc{Defer},
\textsc{Recal}, or \textsc{Abstain} action; none ship a calibrated
OPE estimator.

\paragraph*{Offline RL and OPE methodology}
Conservative Q-Learning~\cite{Kumar2020CQL} and risk-sensitive
distributional variants~\cite{Dabney2018QR-DQN} are now standard;
per-decision importance sampling~\cite{Precup2000PDIS},
doubly-robust estimators~\cite{Jiang2016DR}, and fitted-Q
evaluation~\cite{Le2019BatchRL} are the workhorse OPE estimators.
Our contribution is porting this stack to BCI \emph{and} validating
it against on-policy Monte-Carlo ground truth on a deterministic
per-trial environment.

\paragraph*{EEG foundation models}
\labram{}~\cite{LaBraM2024} (ICLR'24 spotlight, $\sim$2{,}500 hours of
EEG pretraining), CBraMod, and EEGPT release pretrained encoders;
every published downstream usage attaches a supervised classification
head. Our cross-subject experiments (\S\ref{sec:results:labram_loso})
are the first end-to-end RL fine-tuning of an EEG foundation model
in BCI.
